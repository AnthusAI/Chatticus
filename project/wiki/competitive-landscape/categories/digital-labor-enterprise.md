# Digital labor (enterprise)

## What this category means

Enterprise platforms that sell a **digital workforce / digital labor / AI employees** story inside CRM, ITSM, HCM, or general enterprise agent platforms — role-specific agents, orchestration (supervisor / Superagent), human escalation, and governance (control towers, IAM, audit).

Strong on org vocabulary; usually **not** a shared desktop OS / bot-farm computer.

## Why it matters for Chatticus

Enterprise buyers already hear "digital labor," "AI coworkers," and "AI specialists" from Salesforce, ServiceNow, OpenAI Frontier, Atomicwork, Ema. Chatticus can rhyme with that vocabulary, then punch on **shared workplace/computer farm** and non-CRM generalist desks. Do not compete as a CRM or CX agent.
