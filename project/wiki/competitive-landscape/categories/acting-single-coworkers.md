# Acting single coworkers

## What this category means

A **single acting agent** embedded in a product, Slack, or chat — can plan, edit, open PRs, answer from product data — with human merge/approval. Not a farm of named desks. Includes CX "one brand agent" near-misses.

## Why it matters for Chatticus

Medium overlap on acting + memory + tools + approvals. Steal patterns (classifier → act → human merge) for approvals UX; do not become "@bot that opens PRs." CX brands (Sierra, Decagon, Fin) look farm-like in marketing but are customer-facing single agents — out of Chatticus farm peer set.
