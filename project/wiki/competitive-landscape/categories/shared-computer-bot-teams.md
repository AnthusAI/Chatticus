# Shared-computer bot teams

## What this category means

Products where humans and (often named) agents share a **persistent computer** — filesystem, browser, terminal — with skills/routines/approvals, background work, and thin clients. Closest to Chatticus's literal architecture.

## Why it matters for Chatticus

[Grok Bot](../products/grok-bot.md) is the product twin to differentiate against (same computer-sharing model). Zo, Frontier, Dust ("shared virtual computer"), Claude Cowork, Manus, Scout, and Cursor Cloud Agents are adjacent. Trust UX (credential boundaries, wipe semantics, approval cards) is a wedge vs shared-cloud-computer models.
