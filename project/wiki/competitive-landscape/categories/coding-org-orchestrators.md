# Coding-org orchestrators

## What this category means

Frontier multi-agent **coding harnesses / cities of roles** — named roles (Mayor, polecats, standing prod agents), work graphs, mail, merge queues, unattended cron→model. Includes Gas Town → Wheelhouse (Yegge), Beads as dependency, and emergent plans-in-repo blackboard patterns.

## Why it matters for Chatticus

High overlap on specialized desks + work graph metaphor; low–medium on product shape (coding-centric, DIY/bespoke, not household UX). Treat Gas Town as **influential pattern, not stable SaaS competitor**. Coordination substrate choice (Kanbus vs Beads vs blackboard) is a competitive and reliability story.
