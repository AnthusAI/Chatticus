# Sierra / Decagon / Fin

CX near-miss cluster: customer-facing **single agent brands** that look farm-like in marketing but are not multi-desk internal orgs.

| Name | Why it looks farm-like | Why it isn't (vs Chatticus) | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sierra.ai | "Agent OS," enterprise branded agents, multi-channel | Customer-facing single agent brand / managed CX agent | High |
| Decagon | Autonomous support agent platform | CX resolution agent, not roster of internal desks | High |
| Intercom Fin | Strong agent product | Helpdesk-native single support agent | High |

## What it is / who for

Enterprise CX / support buyers. Out of Chatticus farm peer set — do not compete as CRM/CX agent.

## Overlap with Chatticus

**Low (near-miss).** Useful as contrast: Chatticus = **internal multi-desk org** (humans + bots), generalist desks, shared machine — not a branded customer-facing support agent.

## Differentiation

Own internal farm / shared computer; leave CX single-agent brands to Sierra/Decagon/Fin and CRM digital labor to Agentforce.
