# Ema

"Universal AI Employee platform": each **AI Employee** is a **multi-agent mesh** built/orchestrated by Generative Workflow Engine (GWE) across 200+ SaaS apps.

## What it is / who for

Enterprise buyers hiring productized AI Employees (SDR, Agent Assist, etc.) via chat/voice/dashboard/document interfaces and connectors — not a shared computer farm. HITL / guardrails / audit.

## Overlap with Chatticus

**High** on "hire AI employees" roster positioning; enterprise workflow automation. Multi-desk confidence: High (employee metaphor + multi-agent mesh).

## Differentiation

Ema: SaaS-mesh AI Employees. Chatticus: shared workplace/computer, specialized desks, MIT/AWS, household→multi-tenant.
