# Salesforce Agentforce

Salesforce AI agent / digital labor platform: role-specific autonomous agents grounded in CRM + Data 360, with Agent Builder, Atlas Reasoning Engine, and Multi-Agent Orchestration (Superagent → Connected Subagents).

## What it is / who for

Enterprise Salesforce customers across service, sales SDR, sales coach, merchant, employee support, and custom roles. Digital workers inside the Salesforce trust boundary (web/phone/Slack), not a shared desktop OS farm.

## Overlap with Chatticus

**High on vocabulary / org pattern; Medium on literal farm.** Specialized persistent roles; orchestrator → specialists; humans in the loop; "digital labor / digital workforce / agentic enterprise" messaging. Weak overlap on shared computer/tools/files across desks.

Use as the **enterprise vocabulary peer**, then differentiate on shared workplace / computer farm and non-CRM desks.

## Differentiation

Agentforce is CRM/service labor with Superagent MAO (GA; ~7-subagent intent-collision guidance). Chatticus centers shared computer + specialized desks + ownership story. Interop paths Salesforce cites: MAO in-org, A2A, MCP.
