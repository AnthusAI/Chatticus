# Microsoft Copilot Studio

Copilot Studio GA multi-agent orchestration (A2A, Fabric data agents, M365 Agents SDK). Enterprise orchestration platform; Scout is the always-on personal/desktop sibling.

## What it is / who for

Microsoft 365 / Copilot Studio builders connecting multi-agent experiences and Fabric data agents. Multi-desk confidence: Med (platform multi-agent); less "desk farm" UX than Atomicwork/Agentforce vocabulary peers.

## Overlap with Chatticus

**Medium.** Enterprise multi-agent orchestration without Chatticus office/farm UX. [Microsoft Scout](microsoft-scout.md) is closer on computer-use.

## Differentiation

Microsoft platform orchestration vs Chatticus shared-computer specialized desks. Scout (OpenClaw-powered) is the personal always-on angle.
