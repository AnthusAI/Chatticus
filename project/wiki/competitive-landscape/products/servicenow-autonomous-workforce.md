# ServiceNow Autonomous Workforce

Platform "Autonomous Workforce" of domain **AI specialists** that do **jobs not just tasks**, directing teams of AI agents; governed by AI Control Tower; Otto as unified employee AI experience (evolved from Now Assist / Moveworks).

## What it is / who for

Enterprise ServiceNow customers across IT, CRM, employee service, security (specialists expanding 2026). Shared workspace = ServiceNow AI Platform workflows/data — not a desktop farm. Otto is more single front door (near-miss layer); real multi-desk sits in Autonomous Workforce / AI Agents underneath.

## Overlap with Chatticus

**High** on digital employee org chart + orchestration; enterprise ITSM-native. Strong approvals/guardrails/Control Tower bar.

## Differentiation

ServiceNow owns ITSM digital specialists. Chatticus differentiates on shared computer farm and non-ITSM generalist desks. Do not compete as CRM/ITSM agent.
