# Kore.ai

Enterprise agentic platform with multi-agent orchestration (supervisor pattern; ABL `HANDOFF` / `DELEGATE` / `ESCALATE`) for CX and employee experiences.

## What it is / who for

Enterprises building CX/EX agentic applications. Composable specialists; escalation + guardrails in ABL. Enterprise channels/systems — not a bot farm computer.

## Overlap with Chatticus

**Medium.** Orchestration patterns similar (supervisor → specialists); contact-center / EX heritage. Multi-desk confidence: Med.

## Differentiation

Kore: CX/EX platform orchestration. Chatticus: internal multi-desk org, shared computer, non-CX generalist desks. Do not compete as CX agent (see also [Sierra / Decagon / Fin](sierra-decagon-fin.md)).
