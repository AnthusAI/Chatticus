# CrewAI

Agent Management Platform (AMP / Enterprise) for role-based **crews** (multi-agent teams) with Studio, RBAC, audit, HITL, deploy/govern at scale. Framework → platform; not office-desk UX.

## What it is / who for

Developers and automation teams productizing multi-agent orgs as crews. Tools/integrations hub; not a desktop farm. HITL checkpoints; persistent named agents within crews. Also known as OSS multi-agent framework (infra layer).

## Overlap with Chatticus

**Medium** conceptual (roles/crews); different UX layer. Multi-desk confidence: Med. Framework-only view is Low (infra).

## Differentiation

CrewAI: developer/automation AMP. Chatticus: office/farm product for people+bots with shared computer.
