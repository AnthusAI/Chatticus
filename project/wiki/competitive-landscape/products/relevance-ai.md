# Relevance AI

No/low-code **Workforce** canvas: coordinated teams of specialized agents owning a process end-to-end, with triggers, handoffs, tools, and approvals.

## What it is / who for

Teams building AI agent workflows visually. Process canvas + 1000+ app connections — not a full shared OS. Agents reusable across Workforces.

## Overlap with Chatticus

**High** on multi-role teams + human gates; more workflow-automation than "office of bots." Multi-desk confidence: High.

## Differentiation

Relevance: visual workforce automation. Chatticus: shared computer office, named desks as product, authority/approvals beyond canvas gates, ownership story.
