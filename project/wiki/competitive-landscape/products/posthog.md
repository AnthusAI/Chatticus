# PostHog

Product analytics suite shipping PostHog AI (in-app agent), Slack `@PostHog` coding+analytics coworker, MCP, Wizard, and PostHog Desktop (signals → tasks → sandbox PRs) under a "self-driving product" umbrella.

## What it is / who for

PostHog customers (product/eng/ops). Slack app beta for org-wide "@mention → PR or answer"; PostHog AI as product analyst coworker; Desktop aimed at early-stage product engineers (dogfood).

## Overlap with Chatticus

**Medium.** Acting agent with memory, tools, approvals (human merge); Slack as room; can plan/edit/open PRs and babysit CI; product-data context. **Not** a multi-named-bot farm or shared household computer.

## Differentiation

PostHog: deep product telemetry as agent context; one `@PostHog` classifier → sandbox → draft PR; dogfood merged contributions cited in research. Chatticus: multi-desk org, shared workplace, consequential approvals beyond code merge, non-PostHog domains, infra ownership.

Steal the pattern (classifier → act → human merge); do not compete as telemetry-native coding coworker.
