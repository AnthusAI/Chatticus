# Atomicwork

AI-native ITSM/ESM platform that deploys a roster of named **AI Coworkers** (Device Ops, Network Ops, Access Manager, HR Ops, etc.) that own jobs end-to-end with identity, skills, budget, and a human manager.

## What it is / who for

Enterprise IT/HR service management buyers. Explicit "AI Workforce" / Atom AI Coworkers product language. Enterprise tools via MCP; sandboxed execution; coworkers hand off and share service context.

## Overlap with Chatticus

**High** on "hire a roster of specialized digital employees with roles + managers + handoffs." Domain skew: IT/HR service management. Not marketed as one shared desktop computer farm.

Closest non-Salesforce *English* to Chatticus coworker roster messaging (alongside OpenAI Frontier).

## Differentiation

Atomicwork: employee-like identity/budget/manager in ITSM. Chatticus: shared workplace/computer, generalist desks, household→multi-tenant, MIT/AWS ownership. Approvals story must meet the enterprise bar Atomicwork sets with manager governance.
