# Dust

Multiplayer AI for human–agent collaboration: create/share/run many agents on company knowledge + tools; Pods as persistent shared workspaces. Claims shared virtual computer language on product page.

## What it is / who for

Companies wanting multiplayer agents over knowledge and tools. Governance via Spaces/Groups; human review. Claims 300k+ agents / 3k+ orgs (per product materials — treat as vendor claim).

## Overlap with Chatticus

**Med–High.** Strong multiplayer shared workplace; lighter on "specialized desk org chart" branding than Atomicwork/ServiceNow. Also appears in close-peers table as shared-space knowledge assistants.

Pricing secondary ~$29/user (verify).

## Differentiation

Dust: knowledge/tools assistants in Pods. Chatticus: bot farm with specialized desks, shared computer authority system, non-knowledge-only domains.
