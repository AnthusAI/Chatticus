# UiPath Maestro

Low-code Agent Builder + Maestro business orchestration of agents, RPA robots, and humans under one governance layer. RPA → agents lineage.

## What it is / who for

Enterprises with RPA heritage wanting agents + robots + humans in governed BPMN. UI automation across desktops/apps — closest to "agents on computers" among RPA vendors. HITL tasks in Maestro; agents deployed as Orchestrator processes.

## Overlap with Chatticus

**Medium.** Computer-use + human+bot orchestration; less "named desk org" branding. Multi-desk confidence: Med.

## Differentiation

UiPath: RPA/BPMN governance. Chatticus: named specialized desks on shared workplace, non-RPA office metaphor, ownership story.
