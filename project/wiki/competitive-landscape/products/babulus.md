# Babulus

Anthus internal marketing / campaign factory: agents pack; humans click. Complementary to Chatticus — not a competitor.

## What it is / who for

Anthus campaign / outbound production. Chatticus runs bots (bot/agent farm with specialized desks); Babulus is the campaign factory on top of / beside that farm.

## Overlap with Chatticus

**Complementary.** Keep farm ≠ factory in the Anthus stack. Boundary in customer messaging is an open steering question (say the stack out loud vs keep Babulus internal).

## Differentiation

Babulus = campaign factory. Chatticus = bot farm / office / specialized desks. Do not collapse the two metaphors.
