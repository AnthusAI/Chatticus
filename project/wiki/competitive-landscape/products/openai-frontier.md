# OpenAI Frontier

Enterprise platform (announced 2026-02-05) to build/deploy/manage agents as **AI coworkers** with shared business context, computer/tool execution, identity and permissions, and FDE-assisted rollout — not trapped in one app UI.

## What it is / who for

Enterprise customers hiring AI coworkers with IAM, agent identities, permissions/boundaries. Strongest **shared computer + coworker** language among hyperscalers; platform-level rather than Chatticus office UX. Limited availability at announce; broader GA "coming" per OpenAI.

## Overlap with Chatticus

**High** (language + multi-coworker platform); **Med** on whether UX is literally multi-desk roster vs control plane for many agents. Explicit computer and tools access; shared semantic business context.

## Differentiation

Frontier is a hyperscaler control plane with FDE rollout. Chatticus is productized office/farm UX with specialized desks, household v1 path, MIT/customer AWS. Watch Frontier messaging as a positioning mirror.
