# Gas Town / Wheelhouse

Frontier multi-agent coding orchestrator / "city": Gas Town (open-source `gt`, Mayor / polecats / Witness / Refinery, Beads work graph); after Gas Town "fell apart" on Opus 4.7 per Yegge, rebuilt as closed-source Wheelhouse bonded to Wyvern.

## What it is / who for

Power users already running many coding CLIs (Yegge warns Stage 6–8 only). Gas Town = reusable OSS attempt; Wheelhouse = bespoke, "chemically bonded" to the app. Yegge argues reusable harness vendors will fail.

## Overlap with Chatticus

**High on org metaphor.** Named roles; work graph; mail; merge queue; unattended cron→model; specialized desks. **Medium–low on product shape:** coding-centric, tmux/Emacs, Beads dependency, solo-unicorn economics — not a household product UX.

## Differentiation

Yegge: extreme throughput claims (Wheelhouse essay numbers); Fable design → Opus implement → Fable review; standing prod agents; Wish Factory. Chatticus: productized office for people+bots; approvals as product; customer AWS / MIT; not Beads-mandatory; not coding-only.

Treat as **influential pattern, not stable GA SaaS competitor**. Confidence: high on primary essays; do not treat Gas Town as GA SaaS.
