# Public encyclopedia vs this tree

**Date:** 2026-09-04

Two wikis, two jobs. Do not collapse them.

## Kanbus landscape (this directory)

Internal project record. **Categories** plus **one page per product**. Overlap scores, differentiation, freshness, pricing confidence, kill-list notes. Blunt. Not marketing copy.

Entry: [Competitive landscape](index.md). Vocabulary: [Names and metaphors](metaphors.md).

## Public wiki (chattic.us)

`web/content/wiki/` on the marketing site, plus Markus-rendered product docs from `docs/` and `LICENSE`. **Idea pages** plus links to dated blog posts. Not a competitor catalog. Voice is participant and checkable. Published at `/wiki` and linked from the footer.

Editorial IA: `web/content/wiki/AGENTS.md`.
